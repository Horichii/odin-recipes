Pork Adobo

Description

Pork Adobo is a classic Filipino dish known for its rich, savory, and slightly tangy flavor profile. The dish is made by marinating pork in a mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns, then simmering it until the meat is tender and infused with the aromatic sauce. The balance of salty, sour, and umami flavors creates a comforting and hearty meal that is often served with steamed rice. Pork Adobo is celebrated for its simplicity, depth of flavor, and versatility, making it a beloved staple in Filipino cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork shoulder or pork belly, cut into chunks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar (white or cane vinegar)
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine soy sauce, vinegar, minced garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. Add the pork chunks and marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight for best results).
  2. Heat cooking oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Remove the pork from the marinade (reserve the marinade) and brown the pork pieces on all sides.
  3. Once browned, pour in the reserved marinade and add water. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for about 1 to 1.5 hours or until the pork is tender. Stir occasionally.
  5. If desired, add brown sugar to balance the flavors. Adjust salt to taste.
  6. Once the pork is tender and the sauce has thickened to your liking, remove from heat.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.
