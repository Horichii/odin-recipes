Sizzling Pork Sisig

Description

Sisig is a popular Filipino dish made from a combination of pork belly, liver, and heart, seasoned with a spicy and tangy sauce. The meat is typically cooked until crispy and served with a generous amount of the sauce, which is made from vinegar, chili peppers, garlic, and other aromatic ingredients. Sisig is often served with rice and fresh vegetables like lettuce or cabbage.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pork belly, cut into small pieces
  • 1 lb pork liver, cut into small pieces
  • 1 lb pork heart, cut into small pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp vinegar (white or apple cider)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped onions (optional)
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil for cooking

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet or pan over medium-high heat, add the pork belly pieces and cook until crispy and golden brown.
  2. Add the pork liver and heart to the skillet and continue cooking for another 5-7 minutes until fully cooked through.
  3. Add minced garlic to the skillet and cook for an additional minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the vinegar and season with salt and black pepper. Stir well to combine all ingredients.
  5. If using onions, add them to the skillet and cook for another minute until softened.
  6. Serve hot over steamed rice or with fresh vegetables like lettuce or cabbage.
